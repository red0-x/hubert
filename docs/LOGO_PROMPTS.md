# Hubert logo prompts

Hubert is a pink blobfish with round black glasses. Keep the same character across all of these.

**Base character:** a cute pink blobfish with a droopy flat face, small dark oval eyes behind thin round black-rimmed glasses, a pouty mouth and tiny stubby fins.

1. **App icon** (needed: 1024x1024, transparent PNG, readable at 32px)
   > Flat vector app icon of a cute pink blobfish wearing thin round black glasses, centered, bold simple shapes, soft pink with one darker shade for shading, thick clean outline, no text, no background, transparent PNG, front-facing, friendly and slightly grumpy.

2. **Wordmark lockup** (needed: horizontal, transparent, works on dark UI)
   > Logo lockup: the pink bespectacled blobfish mascot on the left, the lowercase word "hubert" on the right in a rounded geometric sans-serif, off-white lettering, minimal modern developer-tool branding, flat vector, transparent background.

3. **Tiny mark** (needed: single color, for favicon and tray)
   > Monochrome silhouette icon of a blobfish with round glasses, single flat color, simple negative-space glasses, 64px readable, vector, transparent background.

4. **Mascot poses** (for the roaming mascot and empty states)
   > Sprite sheet of the same pink blobfish with round glasses: idle, listening with a small headset, thinking with a dot bubble, celebrating, sleeping. Consistent style, flat vector, transparent background, evenly spaced.

Drop files into `docs/` as `logo.png`, `icon.png`, `mark.svg` and tell Hubert's maintainer, or open a PR.
